Feature list
============

Nyxus computes 539 features for 2D images, 213 for 3D volumes and 6 image quality features. Request a feature by its code, or a family of features by its group (see :doc:`featuregroups`).

The *Validation* column says how each feature is tested:

* **vetted** -- the test suite checks its value against an independent implementation,
  published reference values (IBSI) or a closed-form calculation (analytic), named in the
  column;
* **invariant** -- the test suite checks a relation its value must satisfy, such as equality
  between two Nyxus code paths;
* **regression** -- the test suite pins its current value, so a change is noticed, but no
  independent implementation confirms it;
* **untested** -- no test checks its value.

Intensity
^^^^^^^^^

Statistics of the intensities of the ROI's pixels. They are reported in the image's own units; see Intensity units and CT images in the supported formats page.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_pixel_intensity`.

     - vetted: MATLAB

Convex hull
^^^^^^^^^^^

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_morphology`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``CIRCULARITY``
     - Square root of 4 pi times the ROI's pixel count divided by the square of its PERIMETER; close to 1 for a digital disk and smaller for less circular shapes; small or blocky ROIs can exceed 1.
     - vetted: analytic
   * - ``CONVEX_HULL_AREA``
     - Area of the convex hull of the ROI's pixel centres, in pixels, converted to a pixel-count equivalent with Pick's theorem (polygon area plus half the hull boundary lattice points plus 1).

   * - ``SOLIDITY``
     - ROI's pixel count divided by CONVEX_HULL_AREA.
     - vetted: scikit-image
   * - ``POLYGONALITY_AVE``
     - Score of how closely the ROI matches a regular polygon with NUM_NEIGHBORS sides: 10 times the mean of a side-length ratio and an area ratio computed from the ROI's area and PERIMETER. 10 means a perfect match, lower values a poorer one; -1 when the ROI has fewer than 3 neighbors.
     - regression
   * - ``HEXAGONALITY_AVE``
     - Score of how closely the ROI matches a regular hexagon: 10 times the mean of the average pairwise agreement among 11 hexagon area estimates and among 14 hexagon perimeter estimates, built from the area, PERIMETER, convex hull area and minimum and maximum Feret diameters. 10 means a perfect hexagon, lower values a poorer match; -1 when the ROI has fewer than 3 neighbors.
     - regression
   * - ``HEXAGONALITY_STDDEV``
     - Dispersion of the hexagonality estimates: the square root of the mean of the population variances of the pairwise area ratios and of the pairwise perimeter ratios used in HEXAGONALITY_AVE; -1 when the ROI has fewer than 3 neighbors.
     - regression

.. _features-glcm:

Grey-level co-occurrence matrix (GLCM)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

The grey-level co-occurrence matrix counts pairs of ROI pixels one pixel apart, built separately for each angle in ``--glcmAngles`` (default 0, 45, 90 and 135 degrees); pairs with a pixel of stored intensity 0 are skipped, which covers every pixel outside the ROI and any ROI pixel whose value is 0. Each plain code is output as one column per angle, named with the angle as a suffix (for example GLCM_ASM_45), and its _AVE code is the mean of those columns. Grey levels come from ``--coarseGrayDepth`` binning or are the raw intensities under ``--ibsi``; each ordered pair is counted once with a positive grey depth, and the matrix is made symmetric with a negative or zero grey depth and under ``--ibsi``.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_glcm`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``GLCM_ASM``
     - Angular second moment: the sum of the squared co-occurrence probabilities, which is high when a few grey-level pairs dominate (a uniform texture).
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_ACOR``
     - Autocorrelation: the co-occurrence-weighted mean of the product of the two grey levels of a pixel pair, which grows with both coarseness and brightness.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``GLCM_CLUPROM``
     - Cluster prominence: the fourth moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the neighbour pixel's mean grey level, which is large when that distribution is asymmetric or heavy-tailed.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_CLUSHADE``
     - Cluster shade: the third moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the neighbour pixel's mean grey level, which measures the skewness of the co-occurrence matrix.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``GLCM_CLUTEND``
     - Cluster tendency: the second moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the neighbour pixel's mean grey level, which measures how strongly pairs group into similar grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_CONTRAST``
     - Contrast: the co-occurrence-weighted mean squared grey-level difference between the two pixels of a pair, which grows with local intensity variation.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_CORRELATION``
     - Correlation: the linear correlation, from -1 to 1, between the grey levels of the two pixels of a pair.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_DIFAVE``
     - Difference average: the mean absolute grey-level difference between the two pixels of a pair, taken from the distribution of those differences.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_DIFENTRO``
     - Difference entropy: the base-2 entropy of the distribution of absolute grey-level differences between paired pixels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_DIFVAR``
     - Difference variance: the variance of the absolute grey-level difference between paired pixels about its mean.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_DIS``
     - Dissimilarity: the co-occurrence-weighted mean absolute difference between the grey levels of the two pixels of a pair, taken directly from the co-occurrence matrix.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_ENERGY``
     - Energy: the sum of the squared co-occurrence probabilities; it returns the same value as GLCM_ASM.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_ENTROPY``
     - Entropy: the base-2 entropy of the co-occurrence probabilities, which is high when many grey-level pairs are about equally likely; it returns the same value as GLCM_JE.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``GLCM_HOM1``
     - Homogeneity 1: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the grey-level difference of the pair; it returns the same value as GLCM_ID.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_HOM2``
     - Homogeneity 2: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the squared grey-level difference of the pair; it returns the same value as GLCM_IDM and has no _AVE code.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``GLCM_ID``
     - Inverse difference: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the grey-level difference of the pair, which is high when paired pixels have similar grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_IDN``

     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_IDM``

     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLCM_IDMN``

.. _features-glrlm:

Grey-level run-length matrix (GLRLM)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

A run is a straight line of consecutive pixels with the same grey level; runs are collected separately along 0, 45, 90 and 135 degrees into a grey level by run length matrix. Each plain code is output as one column per angle (suffixes _0, _45, _90 and _135), and its _AVE code is the mean of the four. Grey levels come from ``--coarseGrayDepth`` binning, or are the raw intensities under ``--ibsi``. With a positive ``--coarseGrayDepth`` (the default), pixels of stored intensity 0, including every bounding-box pixel outside the ROI, are binned to grey level 1 and form runs; under ``--ibsi`` or a negative ``--coarseGrayDepth`` they are skipped.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_glrlm`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``GLRLM_SRE``
     - Short run emphasis: the mean over all runs of one over the squared run length, which is high when short runs dominate (fine texture).
     - vetted: analytic, PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLRLM_LRE``
     - Long run emphasis: the mean over all runs of the squared run length, which is high when long runs dominate (coarse texture).
     - vetted: analytic, PyRadiomics
   * - ``GLRLM_GLN``

Neighbourhood grey-tone difference matrix (NGTDM)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

For each pixel of nonzero binned grey level that has at least one such neighbour, the matrix accumulates the absolute difference between its grey level and the average grey level of its 8 neighbours (neighbours whose binned grey level is 0 are left out), grouped by grey level, together with the probability of each grey level. Each code is one value per ROI. Grey levels come from ``--coarseGrayDepth`` binning, or are the raw intensities under ``--ibsi``. With a positive ``--coarseGrayDepth`` (the default), pixels outside the ROI are binned to grey level 1 and take part both as centres and as neighbours.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_ngtdm`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``NGTDM_COARSENESS``
     - Coarseness: one over the probability-weighted sum of the differences between each grey level and its neighbourhood averages, which is high when intensity changes slowly (large uniform patches).
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``NGTDM_CONTRAST``
     - Contrast: the spread of the grey levels present multiplied by the mean difference between a pixel and its neighbourhood average, which is high when both the dynamic range and the local variation are large.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``NGTDM_BUSYNESS``
     - Busyness: the probability-weighted neighbourhood differences divided by the summed differences between probability-weighted grey levels, which is high when intensity changes rapidly from pixel to pixel.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``NGTDM_COMPLEXITY``
     - Complexity: the grey-level differences between pairs of levels weighted by their neighbourhood differences, which is high when the image has many abrupt intensity changes.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``NGTDM_STRENGTH``
     - Strength: the probability-weighted squared differences between grey levels divided by the summed neighbourhood differences, which is high when the image has large, distinct regions of different intensity.
     - vetted: MIRP

.. _features-intensity-moments:

Intensity moments
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

The same moments with each pixel weighted by its intensity, in grey levels. The two digits of a code are the powers of x and y. The weighted moments multiply each intensity by ln(d + 0.001), where d is an approximate distance from the pixel to the traced ROI contour, found by a sampled search that can overestimate it.

.. _features-glcm-3d:

GLCM (3D)
^^^^^^^^^

The co-occurrence matrix counts pairs of voxels one voxel apart within the bounding box, built separately for the 13 unique directions of the 26-voxel neighbourhood; pairs touching a voxel of the bounding box outside the ROI are skipped under every binning, and with no binning or under ``--ibsi`` so are pairs touching a ROI voxel of intensity 0. Each _AVE code is the mean over the 13 directions; the plain code is a single value taken from the first direction only (one voxel step along x, y and z together), not an average. Grey levels come from ``--coarseGrayDepth`` binning (overridable with the 3glcm/greydepth metaparameter) or are the raw intensities under ``--ibsi``; each ordered pair is counted once with a positive grey depth, and the matrix is made symmetric with a negative or zero grey depth and under ``--ibsi``.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_glcm_3d`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``3GLCM_ACOR``
     - Autocorrelation: the co-occurrence-weighted mean of the product of the two grey levels of a voxel pair, which grows with both coarseness and brightness.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_ASM``
     - Angular second moment: the sum of the squared co-occurrence probabilities, which is high when a few grey-level pairs dominate (a uniform texture).
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_CLUPROM``
     - Cluster prominence: the fourth moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the mean grey level, which is large when that distribution is asymmetric or heavy-tailed.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_CLUSHADE``
     - Cluster shade: the third moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the mean grey level, which measures the skewness of the co-occurrence matrix.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_CLUTEND``
     - Cluster tendency: the second moment of the sum of the two grey levels of a pair about twice the mean grey level, which measures how strongly pairs group into similar grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_CONTRAST``
     - Contrast: the co-occurrence-weighted mean squared grey-level difference between the two voxels of a pair, which grows with local intensity variation.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_CORRELATION``
     - Correlation: the linear correlation, from -1 to 1, between the grey levels of the two voxels of a pair.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_DIFAVE``
     - Difference average: the mean absolute grey-level difference between the two voxels of a pair, taken from the distribution of those differences.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_DIFENTRO``
     - Difference entropy: the base-2 entropy of the distribution of absolute grey-level differences between paired voxels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_DIFVAR``
     - Difference variance: the variance of the absolute grey-level difference between paired voxels about its mean.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_DIS``
     - Dissimilarity: the co-occurrence-weighted mean absolute difference between the grey levels of the two voxels of a pair, taken directly from the co-occurrence matrix.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_ENERGY``
     - Energy: the sum of the squared co-occurrence probabilities; it returns the same value as 3GLCM_ASM.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_ENTROPY``
     - Entropy: the base-2 entropy of the co-occurrence probabilities, which is high when many grey-level pairs are about equally likely; it returns the same value as 3GLCM_JE.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_HOM1``
     - Homogeneity 1: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the grey-level difference of the pair; it returns the same value as 3GLCM_ID.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_HOM2``
     - Homogeneity 2: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the squared grey-level difference of the pair; it returns the same value as 3GLCM_IDM and has no _AVE code.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_ID``
     - Inverse difference: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the grey-level difference of the pair, which is high when paired voxels have similar grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_IDN``
     - Inverse difference normalized: inverse difference with the grey-level difference divided by the number of grey levels, which makes it less dependent on the number of grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_IDM``
     - Inverse difference moment: the co-occurrence probabilities weighted by one over one plus the squared grey-level difference of the pair, a measure of local homogeneity.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics
   * - ``3GLCM_IDMN``
     - Inverse difference moment normalized: inverse difference moment with the squared grey-level difference divided by the squared number of grey levels.
     - vetted: PyRadiomics

     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``3NGLDM_DCENT``
     - Dependence count entropy: the base-2 entropy of the joint distribution of grey level and dependence count.
     - vetted: MIRP
   * - ``3NGLDM_DCENE``
     - Dependence count energy: the sum of the squared probabilities of the dependence matrix, which is high when a few combinations of grey level and dependence count dominate.
     - vetted: MIRP

.. _features-ngtdm-3d:

NGTDM (3D)
^^^^^^^^^^

For each ROI voxel the matrix accumulates the absolute difference between its grey level and the average grey level of the ROI voxels within the 3ngtdm/radius Chebyshev distance (default 1, at most 26 neighbours), grouped by grey level, together with the probability of each grey level. Each code is one value per ROI. Grey levels come from ``--coarseGrayDepth`` binning (overridable with the 3ngtdm/greydepth metaparameter) or are the raw intensities under ``--ibsi``. Voxels of the bounding box outside the mask are neither centres nor neighbours, in every mode, and a ROI voxel with no ROI neighbour is left out.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_ngtdm_3d`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``3NGTDM_COARSENESS``
     - Coarseness: one over the probability-weighted sum of the differences between each grey level and its neighbourhood averages, which is high when intensity changes slowly (large uniform patches).
     - vetted: MIRP, PyRadiomics
   * - ``3NGTDM_CONTRAST``
     - Contrast: the spread of the grey levels present multiplied by the mean difference between a voxel and its neighbourhood average, which is high when both the dynamic range and the local variation are large.
     - vetted: MIRP, PyRadiomics
   * - ``3NGTDM_BUSYNESS``
     - Busyness: the probability-weighted neighbourhood differences divided by the summed differences between probability-weighted grey levels, which is high when intensity changes rapidly from voxel to voxel.
     - vetted: MIRP, PyRadiomics
   * - ``3NGTDM_COMPLEXITY``
     - Complexity: the grey-level differences between pairs of levels weighted by their neighbourhood differences, which is high when the image has many abrupt intensity changes.
     - vetted: MIRP, PyRadiomics
   * - ``3NGTDM_STRENGTH``
     - Strength: the probability-weighted squared differences between grey levels divided by the summed neighbourhood differences, which is high when the image has large, distinct regions of different intensity.
     - vetted: MIRP, PyRadiomics

Image quality features
----------------------

.. _features-image-quality:

Image quality
^^^^^^^^^^^^^

2D features computed on each ROI's bounding box with pixels outside the ROI set to 0.

Definitions, formulas and settings: :doc:`/Math/f_image_quality`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 30 50 20

   * - Code
     - Description
     - Validation
   * - ``FOCUS_SCORE``
     - Variance of the Laplacian (3x3 kernel) of the ROI's bounding box; higher values mean a sharper image.
     - vetted: OpenCV
   * - ``LOCAL_FOCUS_SCORE``
     - Mean of FOCUS_SCORE over a 2x2 grid of equal, non-overlapping tiles of the bounding box.
     - vetted: analytic, OpenCV
   * - ``POWER_SPECTRUM_SLOPE``
     - Slope of a straight-line fit of log power against log bin index, computed from the image's Fourier transform with each coefficient binned by its own magnitude rather than by its frequency; 0 when the bounding box's shorter side is under 24 pixels.
     - regression
   * - ``MAX_SATURATION``
     - Fraction of bounding-box pixels at the maximum grey level, from 0 to 1.
     - vetted: CellProfiler
   * - ``MIN_SATURATION``
     - Fraction of bounding-box pixels at the minimum grey level, from 0 to 1; pixels outside the ROI count as 0.
     - vetted: CellProfiler
   * - ``SHARPNESS``
     - Edge sharpness from the difference of medians of a 3x3 median-filtered image, combined over the horizontal and vertical directions.
     - regression
